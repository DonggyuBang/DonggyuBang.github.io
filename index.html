About BERL

Engineering better environmental systems.

BERL develops scientific and engineering solutions for circular resource use, sustainable waste management, biological conversion, and environmental protection.

Our Mission

To understand complex environmental and biological processes and translate them into practical technologies for waste reduction, energy recovery, and resource circulation.

Our Approach

We integrate laboratory experiments, microbial ecology, physicochemical analyses, process engineering, and data-driven modeling to address emerging environmental challenges.

Research Areas

What we study.

Our work spans environmental biotechnology, waste valorization, biological treatment processes, and intelligent process optimization.

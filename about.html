About BERL

Environmental engineering across biology, energy, materials, and data.

BERL is a research laboratory in the Department of Earth Resources and Environmental Engineering at Hanyang University. We investigate environmental processes from both mechanistic and systems perspectives, with research spanning bioenergy and resource recovery, environmental remediation, microbial systems, circular materials, organic matter transformation, and data-driven environmental engineering.

Our work connects laboratory-scale experimentation, physicochemical and microbial characterization, process engineering, and computational analysis to better understand how environmental systems transform, recover resources, and respond to changing operating conditions.

Our goal

Advance science that leads to cleaner and more resilient environmental systems.

We aim to translate scientific understanding into practical strategies that improve treatment efficiency, recover valuable resources, reduce environmental impacts, and support sustainable environmental management.

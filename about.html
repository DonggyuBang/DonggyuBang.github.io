About BERL

Bioenergy & Environmental Remediation Lab at Hanyang University.

Mission

Turn environmental complexity into engineered solutions.

BERL investigates biological, chemical, and data-driven mechanisms that enable cleaner treatment, resource recovery, and environmental remediation.

Approach

Mechanism × Process × Translation.

We integrate controlled experiments, microbial ecology, physicochemical characterization, process engineering, and predictive modeling.

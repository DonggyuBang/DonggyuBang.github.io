About BERL

Bioenergy & Environmental Research Laboratory develops sustainable solutions at the intersection of environmental engineering, biotechnology, and resource recovery.

Mission

Environmental research with practical impact.

BERL seeks to understand environmental and biological processes and translate them into technologies for waste reduction, renewable energy, resource recovery, and circular systems.

Approach

Integrated experimentation and data science.

We combine process engineering, microbial ecology, physicochemical analysis, advanced characterization, and machine learning.

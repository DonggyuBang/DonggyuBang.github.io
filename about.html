About BERL

Environmental engineering across biology, chemistry, energy, and AI.

Laboratory identity

Environmental engineering across biology, chemistry, and data.

BERL develops research frameworks that connect environmental mechanisms with practical treatment, resource recovery, and remediation technologies.

Mission

Understand environmental systems deeply enough to engineer them better.

We investigate how microorganisms, materials, organic matter, nutrients, and process conditions interact, then translate those mechanisms into environmental technologies.

Vision

Build circular, resilient, and data-informed environmental processes.

Our long-term direction is to integrate biotechnology, resource recovery, remediation, material transformation, and predictive analytics into sustainable engineered systems.

Research approach

Mechanism → Process → Translation.

BERL research is structured around a repeated four-stage workflow that links fundamental characterization with process-level engineering.

01 · Observe

Characterize

Measure physicochemical properties, microbial responses, molecular changes, and process behavior.

02 · Understand

Resolve mechanisms

Identify interactions and transformation pathways that control system performance.

03 · Engineer

Design processes

Translate mechanisms into pretreatment, biological conversion, remediation, and recovery strategies.

04 · Scale

Predict & optimize

Use process data and modeling to improve robustness, interpretation, and operational decisions.
